Thoughts on the book the Alchemist

Introduction

The Alchemist is a book written by Paulo Coelho. I enjoyed reading this book so much that i actually read another book written by Paulo called the warrior of light. The Alchemist taught me quite a few different things. It is about a boy who goes on a journey. The whole point is to follow your personal legend which is the thing that you want to achieve/accomplish.

Pursuing this legend may involve discomfort and many other things. The thing is that the universe will help you when you actually commit to something and work hard towards it.
